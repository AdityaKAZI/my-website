Exploring Teachers’ Understanding of Adolescent Mental Health and Well-Being: A Phenomenological Inquiry

Master’s Thesis · School of Education, Kathmandu University

This study asks how school teachers understand adolescent mental health and well-being, what they see as their role in supporting students, and how their experiences, beliefs and classroom practices shape that support.

Methodology

  • Approach: Qualitative phenomenological inquiry within a constructivist-interpretivist paradigm
  • Theoretical Lens: Symbolic interactionism
  • Participants: Purposively selected school teachers from Kathmandu and Banepa
  • Data Collection: Semi-structured interviews, informal conversations, observations, and reflective journals
  • Data Analysis: Saldaña’s multi-cycle coding, with standard qualitative quality criteria and ethical safeguards
