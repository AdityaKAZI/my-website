Academic Work & Research.

Inquiry underway

Master’s ThesisOngoing

Exploring Teachers’ Understanding Towards Adolescents’ Mental Health Well-Being: A Phenomenological Inquiry

“A qualitative study of how school teachers understand, interpret and respond to adolescents’ mental health and well-being. Teachers are often the first to notice student distress, yet limited training and scarce school-based psychological services can shape whether a struggling student receives supportive care or disciplinary action. The study aims to uncover the meanings teachers attach to student well-being, the classroom practices that follow, and how to bridge national education policy and everyday school realities in Nepal.”
